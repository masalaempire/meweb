HowLong

A guessing game about time. Estimate how long real things take, from milliseconds to years.

Play the game ↗

The game

How long does an email take to arrive? The game asks questions about real events and lets you answer with a duration. Your guess is scored by its ratio to the answer, so very short and very long times can use the same rules.

The interface

Years, days, hours, minutes, seconds, and milliseconds each have their own controls. Practice lets you choose a category; the daily challenge brings five questions together on a shared board.
