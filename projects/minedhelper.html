MinEDHelper

A place to find the community tools you need in Elite Dangerous.

Open MinEDHelper ↗Source ↗

Elite Dangerous has a lot of community tools. Finding the right one can be a task of its own.

MinEDHelper brings those resources into one searchable directory. You can browse by activity, open common tools, and save useful links.

The Tools page starts with a question: what are we doing today? Route planning, exploration, ship building, and trading each lead to relevant shortcuts. Favorites and personal bookmarks are stored in the browser.
