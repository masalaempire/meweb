OnTimely

A Mac app for planning tasks and remembering when to start, finish, and submit them.

Visit the app site ↗Source on GitHub ↗

From a task to a plan

Add a task to Inbox, give it a deadline, and estimate the work. OnTimely turns that into a plan with a suggested start and a latest safe start. Repeated macOS notifications bring the task back to your attention.

Alongside the reminders

Calendar shows planned tasks by month or day. Notes keeps ideas, links, and lists beside them, and a calendar subscription can bring in school assignments. Tasks are stored on the Mac; no account is needed.
