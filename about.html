How I got here

I got interested in programming the same way I get interested in most things: I wanted to see if I could make something work. At first it was little experiments, then projects, then the familiar “I’ll just fix one more thing” loop.

I still like that part best — the moment a vague idea turns into something you can click, hear, run, or accidentally break. Robotics and hardware are a natural extension of that: more wires, more consequences.

That’s me, more or less.
