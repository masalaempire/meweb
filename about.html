“I like building things because it turns curiosity into something you can actually poke at.”

I got into programming by wondering whether I could make a computer do one small thing, then another, then another. That turned into tools, websites, experiments, and the occasional piece of hardware with more wires than dignity.

The part I still enjoy most is the in-between bit: when something only exists in your head, and you keep nudging it until it starts to work. It doesn’t need to be enormous to be worth making.

on the web

Small sites, real edges.

Cloudflare Pages and Workers are frequent companions. So are too many open tabs.

at the desk

Hardware helps ideas stick.

Piano keys, a turntable, and controls that make an interface feel physical again.

in the margins

Design and books.

Spare industrial forms, Into the Wild, Calvino, Hemingway, and whatever makes a system feel human.

That’s me, more or less.
