I like building things because it turns curiosity into something you can actually poke at.

I got into programming by wondering whether I could make a computer do one small thing, then another, then another. That has turned into little tools, websites, projects, and the occasional piece of hardware with more wires than dignity.

The part I still enjoy most is the in-between bit: when something only exists in your head, and you keep nudging it until it starts to work. It doesn’t need to be enormous to be worth making.

That’s me, more or less.
