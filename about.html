The human behind the tabs

Hi, I’m Simon.

I like making things, learning how stuff works, and getting a little too invested in hobbies. A very normal amount, obviously.

I’m into code, music, machines, and the kind of games where “just one more jump” can turn into a whole evening.

On the code side, I spend a lot of time with Python, C++, and web tech. I like the feeling of taking an idea that is slightly annoying to do by hand and turning it into a tool. Bonus points if it has a nice little interface.

I’m also into robotics and hardware — mostly because making real-world objects listen to your code is still kind of magic. Occasionally the magic is smoke, but that’s part of the learning process.
